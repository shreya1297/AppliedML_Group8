SimpleAverageModel:
A trivial baseline that always predicts the most frequent label in the training set.
Used to test the end-to-end ML pipeline (data loading, preprocessing, batching, evaluation, submission).

This model performs no training and simply predicts the most common label from the training dataset for every example.
Validation Accuracy: 0.2349

Submission columns:
- id: taken directly from test.csv
- label: model predictions (constant = most common label)

Example:
id,label
test_0,1
test_1,1
...

- This model does not use any training.
- Logits are manually constructed to prioritize one label.
- Accuracy is expected to be low but helps verify pipeline correctness.
